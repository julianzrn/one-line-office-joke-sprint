# One-Line Office Joke: 24-Hour Sprint — 要求与候选

## 官方要求（核对自 make.fun 公开接口，2026-11-25）
- slug: u-pucuys-one-line-office-joke-24-hour-sprint-aaf86162
- 形式：单行办公室笑话，最多 200 字符；就是那种「这会本该是一封邮件」之后发给同事的段子。
- 评判：house judge 按公开 brief 两两对比，更好笑的胜出；奖池分前十名。
- 截止：2026-11-25T05:50:00Z；24 小时冲刺，私下轮次评判。

## 候选
1. The meeting could have been an email, but the email would have needed a meeting to explain it, so here we all are.
2. Our standup is fifteen minutes long: two minutes of updates and thirteen of one man saying 'can you see my screen?'
3. I love my job: the printer is the only colleague who tells me exactly what's wrong, in writing, immediately.
4. We replaced the water cooler with a Slack channel, and somehow the gossip got version control.
5. HR says we're a family here, which explains why nobody gets paid and everyone knows what I did at the Christmas party.
6. The boss said 'let's circle back,' so we did — we've been circling for three fiscal years and morale is a roundabout.

## 选定
第 1 句。直接咬住 brief 的「本该是封邮件」，再套一层递归反转：邮件又需要开会解释——意外、具体、朗读顺口。
